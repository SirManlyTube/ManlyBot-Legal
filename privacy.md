# Privacy Policy for ManlyBot

*Last updated: October 2026*

## 1. Information Collected
ManlyBot processes limited data required to function on Discord:
* **Discord Identifiers:** User IDs, Server (Guild) IDs, and Channel IDs to manage configurations and commands.
* **User Input:** Text entered specifically via slash commands or bot-targeted interactions.
* **Stored Data:** No data is permanently stored.

## 2. How We Use Data
Collected data is used strictly to provide, maintain, and improve the bot's features. We do not sell, license, or share user data with third parties or data brokers.

## 3. Data Retention and Removal
* Data is stored only as long as necessary to provide service.
* Users can request the removal of their stored data by joining our support server at https://discord.gg/wsPkNpGd6x or contacting us at manlytubecontact@gmail.com.

## 4. Revisions
This policy may be updated periodically. Continued use of the bot signifies acceptance of any changes.
